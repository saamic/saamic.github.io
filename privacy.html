Privacy Policy for Gatho

Last Updated: 11/11/25

Introduction

Gatho ("we," "our," or "us") is a social event planning and group communication app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Phone Number: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Profile Information: Name, birthday, profile picture, bio, and location
  • Content: Events you create, messages you send, and photos you upload
  • Contacts: With your permission, we access your contacts to help you invite friends to events

Information We Collect Automatically

  • Location Data: If you enable automatic location tracking, we update your city location approximately every 30 minutes to help friends see where you are and discover nearby events. You can disable this in your profile settings.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the app and its features
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for troubleshooting

Device Permissions

  • Calendar Data: With your permission, we can access your calendar to help schedule events
  • Camera and Photos: With your permission, we access your camera and photo library to upload profile pictures and event posters

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable you to create events (Gathos) and communicate with other users
  • Connect you with friends and help you discover nearby users and events
  • Send you notifications about event invitations, messages, and updates
  • Improve app functionality and user experience
  • Provide customer support

How We Share Your Information

With Other Users

  • Your profile information (name, profile picture, location) is visible to other Gatho users
  • Events you create or attend may be visible based on privacy settings you choose
  • Messages you send are visible to other members

With Service Providers

We work with third-party service providers who help us operate Gatho, including cloud hosting, SMS verification, mapping services, and other technical infrastructure. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for providing services to Gatho.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Update or correct your profile information within the app
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Disable automatic location tracking at any time
  • Control who can see your events through privacy settings

To delete your account or exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@gatho.co with your request. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure authentication via SMS verification
  • Secure database access controls
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Gatho is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via your registered phone number.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we must retain it for legal obligations.
